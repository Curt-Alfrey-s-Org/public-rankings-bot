# Rankings Bot Account Setup - Quick Start for Players

Welcome! You've been added to the Rankings Bot system. Follow these simple steps to get started.

---

## Your Account Credentials

You should have received a CSV file with the following information:

```
username,password
john_smith,X9#kL2@mP5!
```

**Save this file securely** — you'll need these credentials to log in for the first time.

---

## Step 1: First Login

1. Go to **https://rankings.yourdomain.com** (or the link provided)
2. Click **Login**
3. Enter your **username** (e.g., `john_smith`)
4. Enter your **temporary password** from the CSV file
5. Click **Sign In**

---

## Step 2: Set Your Email (Required)

After logging in, you'll be prompted to set your email address.

1. Enter your **email address** (e.g., `john@example.com`)
2. Click **Save**

⚠️ **Why email is required:**
- Password reset functionality depends on it
- Account recovery if you forget your password
- Tournament notifications and updates

---

## Step 3: Change Your Password

Now set a password you can remember:

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security** (or **Change Password** button)
2. Your email address is already confirmed
3. Choose a **new password** (8+ characters recommended)
4. Click **Save Password**

From now on, use **your new password** to log in.

---

## Step 4: Customize Your Profile (Optional)

Make your profile name match how you're known in pool:

1. Go to **Profile** → **Profile Customization**
2. Set your **Display Name** (nickname) — this is how others see you in rankings
   - Leave blank to use your username
   - Example: "Johnny" instead of "john_smith"
3. Click **Save**

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Account Locked" Error
- You've entered the wrong password 5 times in a row
- Wait **30 minutes** and try again
- Contact an admin to unlock it immediately

### "Forgot Password"
- Go to **Login** → **Forgot Password**
- Enter the **username** you were assigned
- Click **Send Reset Link**
- Follow the link sent to your **email** (the one you set in Step 2)
- Set a new password

### "Login Won't Work"
- Double-check your **username** (case doesn't matter, but spelling does)
- Use your **new password** (not the temporary one from the CSV)
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Contact admin for support

---

## Need Help?

If you have any issues:
1. Check if "Account Locked" (see above)
2. Try the "Forgot Password" flow
3. Contact the admin or tournament director
