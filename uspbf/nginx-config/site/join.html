Accounts & who is tracked

example.com is the federation’s public home for news, rankings snapshots, players, tournaments, and rules. Pro Billiards Software at probilliardssoftware.com is where you will sign in, register for events, and use the full tools USPBF runs on once accounts open. Account creation is not available yet. Existing WPA pros with director-issued credentials may sign in when instructed.

Professional players (WPA license)

Your login account is created for you from your WPA license number when you are provisioned in the system (import, director setup, or admin). Use the username and instructions your tournament director or USPBF provides, then sign in at probilliardssoftware.com.

If you have a WPA license and do not yet have an account, do not use the public self-registration form—contact your director or USPBF so your record can be linked correctly.

Top-level amateurs

Self-service account creation is coming soon. Tournament results and rankings for top-level amateur play can be tracked when directors and the federation designate eligibility (e.g. director-verified or association criteria). When signup opens, you’ll be able to enter an association license number so we can try to link your profile automatically.

Other players & handicap divisions

Self-service account creation is coming soon. Handicap-based tournament divisions and full rankings tracking for lower skill bands are not finished yet—that work is in progress. When signup opens, the same account will apply.
